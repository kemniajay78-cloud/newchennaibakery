# 🍰 Chennai Bakery

A modern, responsive bakery e-commerce website designed for **Chennai Bakery**, offering customers an easy way to explore bakery products, customize cakes, add items to a cart, place orders, and interact with an AI-powered bakery assistant.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🛍️ Customer Shopping

- Browse bakery products
- Product categories and filtering
- Product search
- Product details
- Veg / Non-Veg classification
- Eggless product identification
- Product images
- Product ratings
- Add to Cart
- Update cart quantities
- Remove products from cart
- Order checkout
- Pickup and local delivery options

### 🎂 Custom Cake Studio

Customers can create a customized celebration cake by selecting:

- Cake size
- Sponge
- Flavour
- Filling
- Frosting
- Design
- Piping/message
- Special requirements

The website provides a live cake customization experience.

---

## 🤖 Chennai Bakery AI Assistant

The website includes an AI-powered shopping assistant designed to help customers find products and complete their shopping journey.

### AI capabilities

- Product recommendations
- Natural-language product search
- Budget-based recommendations
- Eggless product recommendations
- Veg / Non-Veg filtering
- Product information
- Product images inside chat
- Add products to cart
- Cart assistance
- Custom cake assistance
- Order assistance
- Delivery information
- Bakery FAQs
- Human support handoff

Example:

> "I need an eggless chocolate cake for 10 people under ₹1,000."

The AI can recommend suitable products from the bakery catalogue.

### Important AI principle

The AI should use the actual bakery catalogue as its source of truth.

It should never invent:

- Products
- Prices
- Stock
- Offers
- Delivery availability
- Order status
